Auto Clip Farm

Privacy Policy

Effective August 11, 2026

Auto Clip Farm is a locally operated Windows desktop application. This policy describes the information used when a user connects a social media account or uploads a clip.

Information used

The application may receive an account identifier, display name, authorization tokens, and the permissions granted by the user. For a requested upload, it processes the selected video file and user-provided post information such as a title, description, caption, hashtags, tags, privacy selection, and interaction settings.

How information is used

This information is used only to connect the requested account, refresh authorization when permitted, upload the selected content, report upload status, and let the user disconnect the account. Auto Clip Farm does not sell personal information or use connected-account data for advertising.

Storage and sharing

Application state and account credentials are stored on the user's computer. Selected videos and metadata are transmitted directly to the destination chosen by the user, such as YouTube or TikTok, and are then governed by that platform's terms and privacy policy. Auto Clip Farm does not operate an independent cloud database for social-account data.

Retention and deletion

Local upload jobs and credentials remain until the user removes them, disconnects the account, or deletes the application's local data. Disconnecting an account revokes or removes its stored authorization where supported. Content already delivered to a social platform must be managed or deleted through that platform.

Security

Auto Clip Farm uses platform authorization flows and limits requested permissions to the functions the user enables. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and users should protect access to their Windows account and connected social accounts.

Children

Auto Clip Farm is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the application changes. Questions or privacy requests may be submitted through the project's public GitHub Issues page.
